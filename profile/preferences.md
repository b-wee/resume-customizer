# Resume preferences

Personal rules `/tailor` applies to every resume. Edit to taste — these
override the defaults written in the tailor skill.

## Writing style

- No em-dashes and no semicolons anywhere in resume text. Use commas, periods,
  or parentheses instead.

## Bullets

- Maximum 6 bullets per job.

## Confidential financials

- Never put absolute revenue, budget, or cost figures from private companies
  on a resume. Use percentages, growth multiples, relative framings
  (8-figure), headcount, users, or volume instead.
- Publicly reported figures are allowed.
